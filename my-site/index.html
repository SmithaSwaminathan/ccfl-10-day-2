01 About

TA built on procurement literacy, not keyword matching

I'm Smitha, a Talent Acquisition specialist with 19+ years of experience — 15 of them at Beroe Inc., a global procurement intelligence firm serving Fortune 500 clients. I own end-to-end hiring for Beroe's Research function: from building the JD to closing the offer, across Analyst and Senior Analyst roles in 15+ category specialisations.

What makes this different: I assess procurement literacy, not just credentials. I know what a strong Pharma research analyst looks like versus a strong Chemicals one. I know when a candidate understands supplier landscapes versus when they're repeating terminology. I evaluate culture-fit alongside technical competence — because a hire that doesn't last isn't a hire.

I came into procurement knowing nothing about it. That turned out to be an advantage: having to learn it properly means I can now test whether a candidate genuinely understands it — something most TA professionals simply can't do.

03 What I Offer

The capabilities that
close roles well.

01

Domain-Specific Hiring

Analyst and Senior Analyst roles across Pharma/Life Sciences, Industrials, Chemicals, Energy, IT, HR Services, Facilities Management, Financial Risk, and more. I hire for categories I know — which means I ask the right questions and recognise the right answers.

02

Procurement Literacy Assessment

Beyond the CV: I assess a candidate's understanding of market intelligence, supplier analysis, price forecasting, and category research frameworks — the substance behind the skills list. Keywords are not fluency.

03

Culture-Fit Screening

Technical competence is the floor. I evaluate attitude, learning orientation, and organisational fit — the factors that determine whether a hire succeeds in the long run. A hire who doesn't fit doesn't last, regardless of credentials.

04

JD Architecture

Structured, consistent, domain-calibrated job descriptions that bring in the right candidates and reduce screening noise. Built to reflect real role expectations, not template language. No buzzwords. No copy-paste.

05 The Process

No ghosting.
No loose ends.

01

Intake

I meet with the Research Manager or Domain Lead to understand what they actually need — not just what's in the brief. The job spec is a starting point, not a destination.

02

Sourcing

Targeted identification of talent across channels, with focus on domain-relevant profiles and procurement-adjacent backgrounds. Quality over volume, always.

03

Assessment

Technical briefing and cultural evaluation. Both matter equally. A candidate who can't fit the team won't deliver, regardless of how strong the CV looks.

04

Coordination

Panel scheduling, feedback loops, HR evaluation, offer management. Smooth handoffs at every stage so nothing falls through.

05

Closure

Every candidate — placed or not — receives clear, specific feedback. If it's a no, they know what to work on and when to come back. I close doors without closing relationships.
