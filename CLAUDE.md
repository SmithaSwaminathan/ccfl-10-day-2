## Lead Scoring Rules

Score inbound leads HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW based on the following criteria. Be specific — a generic "we need researchers" is not the same as "we need a Pharma Analyst who can do supplier landscape mapping."

### HIGH — Act fast, prioritize the proposal

All of the following should be true (or mostly true):

**Who they are**: A procurement intelligence firm, market research company, procurement consultancy, or a large enterprise (Fortune 500 / large Indian conglomerate) with an established procurement/category management research function. Companies like Beroe, Gartner, McKinsey Procurement, Accenture Strategy, or a Fortune 500 CPO office building an in-house research team.

**What they need**: Analyst or Senior Analyst roles — the exact profiles Smitha hires. Domain must fall within her coverage: Pharma/Life Sciences, Industrials (Capex & MRO, Engineering, IMD), Chemicals & Energy, Services (FM, Professional Services, HR, Marketing Agencies), IT & Telecom, or Quantitative (Price Forecasting, Financial Risk).

**Volume or urgency**: Two or more open roles, or a single role where a generalist agency has already failed to deliver the right profile. A hiring drive (quarterly or annual target) is a strong signal.

**Geography**: Roles based in India (Chennai, Bangalore, or Remote India) or CEE (Central & Eastern Europe). These are Smitha's active hiring markets.

**Budget**: Consistent with market rates for procurement research — INR 6–15 lakhs CTC for Analysts, INR 12–25 lakhs for Senior Analysts, or equivalent. A company that knows the number is a company that's done this before.

**Decision-maker**: Research Manager, TA/HR Head, BU Lead, or Associate Director-level stakeholder reaching out directly — not a junior coordinator shopping around.

---

### MEDIUM — Send a tailored proposal, follow up once

One or two HIGH criteria are missing, but the core need is real:

- Company is building a procurement research function from scratch — the need is genuine but the hiring plan is still forming
- Single role with no immediate urgency, or timeline is "next quarter" rather than now
- Adjacent domain: supply chain consulting, FMCG with a category intelligence team, management consulting with a procurement practice — transferable but not Smitha's core
- Generalist TA is in place but struggling; the company knows something's wrong but hasn't committed to a specialist yet
- Budget mentioned but vague ("market rate" without specifics) or slightly below range
- Geography is India but role scope or level is unclear

---

### LOW — Send the proposal, no active follow-up

Flag for records. Don't invest time chasing:

- **Individual job seeker** contacting through the proposal flow — redirect to smitha@beroe-inc.com and point them to the active hiring drive
- Company outside procurement research entirely: pure B2B SaaS, e-commerce, FMCG without a research/intelligence function, fintech, healthcare delivery (not pharma research)
- Company too small to support structured TA (under 20 people) — no hiring process to plug into
- Role mismatch: sales, marketing, engineering, operations — not Analyst/Senior Analyst research profiles
- Geography mismatch: wants US, UK, or Middle East hiring only, with no India/CEE component
- No real hiring need — competitor benchmarking, academic curiosity, or a "just exploring" inquiry with no role, no timeline, no budget
